Second Year · Core Course

Computer System Architecture

A course focused on digital logic, sequential circuits, registers, instruction formats, addressing modes, microoperations, and input-output organization.

Core Course

Course Introduction

This course helps students understand the internal architecture of computer systems, from digital logic foundations to processor organization, instructions, memory-related ideas, and input-output transfer mechanisms.

Second Year

Learning Objectives

Students will learn combinational and sequential logic, processor organization, register transfer, instruction formats, addressing modes, assembly-level ideas, and I/O architecture concepts.
